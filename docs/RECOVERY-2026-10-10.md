## Isolation, bounded execution and preserved failures

Only the pinned cached TimescaleDB 2.29.1 / PostgreSQL 16 image is used. Containers
have no network/ports, a readonly root, dropped capabilities, no-new-privileges,
one CPU, 4 GiB memory with no extra swap, 3 GiB PGDATA tmpfs, bounded CLI captures,
Windows assign-before-resume job supervision and independent Linux PID1 watchdogs.
The accepted native workflow took 318.328 seconds. All owned containers were
removed; the hidden supervisor verifies zero remaining owned Windows processes.
Its successful v8 lease alone was removed; historical failed leases survive.

Docker cannot export the required dump from a tmpfs mount through `docker cp`.
This was reproduced on a small public fixture. The owned source/restore copies
therefore use private writable host-bind `/tmp` directories, while the cold source
bind remains readonly. The official backup body and original transport are
unchanged. An observed 128 MiB scratch ceiling is checked during the official
process and around native calls; the peak was 79,014,806 bytes. This is an
observer ceiling, **not a hard filesystem quota**. Three private scratch
directories and their diagnostic/backup artifacts are retained as evidence;
container cleanup is not a claim that all disk scratch was deleted.

The earlier capacity, extraction-metadata, interrupted-supervisor, SQL-temp-space,
Compose-discovery and tmpfs-export failures are preserved fail-closed evidence,
not successful backups. Their owners, leases, source fingerprints and receipts
were not adopted, reset, rewritten or removed. Trial 15, V2, V4/V5 and unfinished
