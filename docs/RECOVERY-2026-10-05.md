## Why the official manifest was not produced

The failed backup's injected host transport stopped on rendered source line 33:
its fixed native-call cap was 26. All 26 journaled calls completed with exit zero;
call 24 dumped, 25 copied and 26 removed its own container scratch. Call 27 was
rejected before its dispatch intent, so post-backup checkpoints never ran.

The unchanged official backup body requires **47 calls**: two discovery calls,
19 table counts plus a sequence and owner query before the dump, three
dump/copy/cleanup calls, and the same 21 provenance calls afterward. The dump
can restore while the official backup workflow still correctly lacks acceptance.

An additive, unarmed v6 repair candidate under
`D:\Kairos\runtime\archive-clone-20261005` changes the audited cap to 47 without
editing historical v4/v5 transport or backup source, or reusing the failed owner.
It preserves the original failure-path `finally` cleanup. The candidate has no
native backup entry point, chain loader or lease-adoption authority.

All **24 offline tests passed**, including a Windows PowerShell fake-native
47-call flow, rejection of call 48 before dispatch/intent, and cleanup after
failed dump and failed copy. These fixtures made zero Docker/database calls:
[offline qualification](receipts/runtime-backup-transport-offline-20261005.json),
[detached signature](receipts/runtime-backup-transport-offline-20261005.json.asc).
