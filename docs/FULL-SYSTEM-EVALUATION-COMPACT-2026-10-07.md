# Compact context-assisted complex: pre-model engineering

This is a **new research-only hypothesis**, not a selected profitable strategy,
production integration or frozen-campaign revision. Trial 15, V4/V5, the old
adaptive evaluator, all prior tapes/receipts and the review-only A/D pilot remain
unchanged. No model historical test is authorized by this engineering work.

## Fixed trading concept

`compact_context_complex_v1` combines two existing, unmodified native defaults:

- `trend_breakout_v1`: trend continuation, with its exact 54-hour origin and
  **expanding** native EMA/Wilder prefix.
- `adaptive_pullback_range_v1`: confirmed pullback, range-edge reclaim and the
  controlled crash-retest SHORT, with its original **rolling** 3,240 closed 1m
  bars and original 60-second entry lifetime.

The native crash detector and exact two-completed-post-shock-hour recovery rule
protect the entire complex: breakout cannot bypass them. Same-direction native
collisions keep adaptive first and the unchanged selected candidate once;
opposite directions become a distinct `CONFLICT`, not ordinary quiet. Dropped
alternatives are recorded and never resurrected after a veto or execution refusal.
The slower right-tail pair remains a reference, not an intraday entry engine.

There is no daily trade quota, no forced entry, no fixed income target and no
claim that one price pattern must supply all of the system's economic edge.

## One context assessment, separate counterfactual uses

The new fixed protocol retains the four original A/D cuts across BTC, ETH, SOL,
BNB and XRP: **20 cells total**. It is a sparse retrospective diagnostic, not
continuous five-day trading, representative monthly performance or a blind pass.
Other slots are not evaluated and are not imputed quiet.

One underlying call per scheduled cell supplies a strict joint response:
`long_review`, `short_review` (`ALLOW/VETO/DEFER`), `proposal` (`NONE/LONG/SHORT`)
and allowed causal evidence references. Prices, stops, targets, size, leverage,
confidence and extra fields are prohibited. A directional proposal whose own
review is not `ALLOW` invalidates the **whole** response; no favorable field is
salvaged. Invalid references, duplicate fields, refusal or incomplete output
fail closed. Costs are retained, including unknown/held expenses.

The model sees causal market prices (last 30 completed 1m, 24 completed 5m,
16 completed 15m and 53/54 completed 1h bars), versioned NEWS and MACRO only.
It sees no technical candidate, strategy state, generated regime, position
geometry, outcome, full future archive digest, future funding or other arm output.
Calls are scheduled independently of whether the technical slot has a candidate.

This is **directional context assessment**, not candidate-specific native review
and not two statistically independent model roles. The same response is reused
for technical-only, context-review, independent-proposal, combined and a
review-timing control. Uncertainty must be grouped by assessment/cut, not treat
correlated accounts as independent model trials.

## Deterministic proposal geometry and conflicts

`context_atr_proposal_v1` maps a model direction using only the exact closed
54-hour price history: finite ATR14 of completed 15m bars, 1.25 ATR stop, 2R
target, minimum/maximum structural stop bounds 0.5/2 ATR, maximum 300 bps stop,
fixed planning cost 20 bps, cost/stop at most 0.5 and net reward/risk at least
1.25. Its own five-minute lifetime is a **new explicit mapper policy**; it never
extends or replaces the old adaptive candidate's 60-second lifetime. Holding
is 120 minutes, or 60 in range. These are assumptions, not measured venue facts.

An ordinary quiet slot can contain an independently context-originated proposal
without requiring another technical entry pattern. During crash, only the
unchanged original controlled-retest SHORT can map; post-shock cooldown abstains.
The model never provides executable geometry or risk sizing.

Combined policy remains strategy-first: baseline `VETO/DEFER` blocks entry;
opposite eligible directions abstain; same direction keeps the original baseline
once; genuine quiet can admit the mapped proposal. Failed/missing mapping cannot
be silently changed to `NONE` to rescue a baseline. A technical `CONFLICT` remains
an abstention in combined; the isolated proposal account is evaluated separately.

## Expenses, clocks, execution and authority

The route remains OpenAI `gpt-6-luna`, medium, at most 2,048 output tokens,
zero retries. The new protocol receives **no additional $1/20-call allocation**:
it shares the old immutable local pilot ledger as cumulative ceiling authority,
intersecting the existing $12 authoritative OpenAI budget and prior/held usage.
The old ledger/draft identity is not the new assessment protocol identity. A
separate new-protocol/source/budget admission and fresh owner confirmation are
mandatory; no ledger is created or reset by preparation.

Actual request/response/cost clocks remain modern. An explicitly labelled
reconstruction projects their elapsed times after the fixed 100 ms baseline
delay. The measured request begins at dispatcher entry, before reservations,
prompt construction and native provider dispatch: local preparation/queue time
cannot be omitted from the successful response path. One separately timestamped
mapper receipt is reused across all execution
modes and cost scenarios. Context review and its timing control share the same
effective clock; combined waits for mapping and rechecks original expiry.
Held NEWS/MACRO coverage, version identity and freshness are rechecked at the
effective quote clock. Later context can cause `DEFER` only, never enrich the
original prompt or revise the model decision.

Each model-using hypothetical account debits one underlying charge once, before
same-clock sizing; combined does not pay twice. Technical-only is the no-model
counterfactual. Account expenses are not additive physical API expenditure.
Missing receipts or unknown expenses null the affected full economics; known
physical expenses remain explicitly a lower bound, not an assumed total.

All accounts use unchanged `COMMON_COST_RISK_V1`, fees/funding/costs, strict
minute-open versus explicitly unobserved intrabar proxy, original barriers and
bounded exits. Risk is at most 0.25% per admission and 1% aggregate admission,
25% per-symbol notional, 1x gross. These are admission limits, not a guarantee
against later MTM ratio changes or gap losses. No model confidence uplift applies.

## Pre-test boundary

The new `kairos-compact-context-prepare` command is create-only and strictly
offline, one worker / 300 seconds. It seals the protocol before preparing the
exact 20 cells, checks cached FULL_KLINE/funding inputs and installed immutable
dependencies, writes hash-bound prompts and verifies source equality afterward.
It has **no model-run flag**, credential loader, database connection, budget
initialization or order path. Partial failures remain retained, never overwritten.

An archive checksum/schema pass does not establish source authenticity, exact
historical versions, local historical receipt or comprehensive news coverage.
The existing CPI vintage extractor is not a qualified full NEWS/MACRO corpus.
Absent an accepted source corpus, source readiness stays unavailable and model
economics stays `null`. Owner confirmation alone does not manufacture that proof.

The model test will run only after a fresh owner confirmation and accepted
source/budget admission. Modern retrospective model results cannot exclude
pretrained historical-event memory and earn zero blind-campaign days. No new
paid API calls, primary recovery, consumers, venue, PAPER or LIVE are operated.
All readiness remains false; `STRATEGY_POLICY=REJECT_ALL`.

The [actual preparation receipt](FULL-SYSTEM-EVALUATION-COMPACT-RESULT-2026-10-07.md)
records completed engineering checks separately from unavailable historical
NEWS/MACRO admission and the model test that has not been run.

Structured response handling follows the official
[OpenAI Structured Outputs guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs).
It is an interface constraint, not model-quality or profitability evidence.
